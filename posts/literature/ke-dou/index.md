<div class="poem">
<p class="poem__line">清潭里游着波动的黑</p>
<p class="poem__line">微波轻漾</p>
<p class="poem__line">阳光初暖</p>
<p class="poem__line poem__line--stanza">细砂里裹着稚嫩的白</p>
<p class="poem__line">笑声所及</p>
<p class="poem__line">鸟动林吹</p>
<p class="poem__line poem__line--stanza">隔着细碎的水面</p>
<p class="poem__line">白眼儿瞅对着黑点儿</p>
<p class="poem__line">披上流泛的粼光</p>
<p class="poem__line">黑点儿铆劲逆着波流</p>
<p class="poem__line poem__line--stanza">节节败退！</p>
<p class="poem__line">节节败退！</p>
<p class="poem__line">这是来自镜面外</p>
<p class="poem__line">密度稀薄的成功</p>
<p class="poem__line poem__line--stanza">及此</p>
<p class="poem__line">草丛边跳出一抹灵动的绿影</p>
<p class="poem__line">小白眼儿分散了注意</p>
<p class="poem__line">小黑点儿</p>
<p class="poem__line">恁地</p>
<p class="poem__line">伸出了四肢</p>
<p class="poem__line poem__line--stanza">纵身一跃</p>
<p class="poem__line">蝉鸣乍止</p>
<p class="poem__line">巴掌大的鱼塘</p>
<p class="poem__line">竟响起了命运的浪潮</p>
<p class="poem__line poem__line--stanza">呱！</p>
<p class="poem__line">呱……</p>
</div>
